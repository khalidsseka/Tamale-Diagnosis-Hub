Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you diagnose a car remotely?

Yes. For online diagnosis, send the vehicle details, fault codes, scanner screenshots and symptoms. We can guide you through the next tests.

Do I need a VCDS scanner?

No. VCDS is especially useful for VW/Audi, but other OBD scanner information can also be reviewed where the data is sufficient.

Should I replace the part shown by the fault code?

Not automatically. A fault code identifies a system or condition; testing is often needed to determine whether the component, wiring, power supply or another condition is the real cause.
